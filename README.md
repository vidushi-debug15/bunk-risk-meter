Bunk Risk Meter
Most attendance trackers tell you your percentage. This one tells you the thing you actually want to know: how many more classes can I skip?
Try it: https://vidushi-debug15.github.io/bunk-risk-meter/
What it does
- Add your subjects with classes held and attended. You can edit or delete them anytime.
- Each subject gets a card showing how many classes you can still miss while staying above the cutoff (75% by default, and you can change it).
- Cards are colour-coded. Green means you're comfortable, yellow means you're running thin, and red means you're below the cutoff and need to start attending.
- You get an alert when a subject turns red.
- A "what if I skip" slider shows what happens to your attendance before you bunk.
